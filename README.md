## How to read it

Honda row: a kit made for the 2025 CRF450R also fits the 2025 to 2026 CRF450R and CRF250R.

Not covered: aftermarket plastics (Acerbis, Polisport, UFO), which usually but not always follow the stock shape, and small mid-year changes.

## Contributing

Free to use. Found a mistake or a missing fitment? Open an issue with the model, the years and your source.
